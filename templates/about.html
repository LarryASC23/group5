About this shop

Shady Shop is a parody storefront built for CS 3773 (Software Engineering) at UTSA, Fall 2026. It looks like a "dark market" site, but every product is a joke and nothing is real.

  • All items are fictional.
  • Checkout records a demo order; no payments are processed.
  • Built with Django, SQLite, Bootstrap 5, and vanilla JavaScript.
